Outline a cyber security course - for you.
(CyberSecurity Reality Lab - Think Like an Attacker, Act Like a Defender)
  Goals (2-5. After the course, the student...)
  
  - Can design practical security measures, by using concepts like MFA, logging, backups, incidents response etc.
  - Can communicate cybersecurity risk to different audiences. These audiences could be management team, end users, administrators or business owners.
  - Can identify and assess realistic risks of cybersecurity by considering assets, business impact, existing controls, threats and vulnerabilities.
  - Can investigate and response to simulated cyber incidents with the help of using logs and other technical evidence.
  - Can evaluate an organization's cybersecurity maturity with the help of using different frameworks.

  Outline/Agenda (what's the theme each time)
  - Identity is the new perimeter
  - Think like an attacker
  - Incident day
  - Build and break a small environment
  - Cybersecurity meets the business

  Exercises (No need to figure out the technical details, you can just describe them or give examples. 
  You don't need to be able to solve those exercises. Are they written, hands-on labs, group work, facilitated discussions, roleplay, capture-the-flag...)
  - Hands on exercises with different aspects like: Conditional access, MFA, Passkeys & Passwords, Phishing + Privileged accounts
  - Making an fictional company, receiving information about its business processes, cloud services, environment, servers, users and suppliers. After that answering the question:
    "“You want access to the company's most important information. Where would you attack?”"
  - Investigating an incident, where an company is under attack. Investigate → contain → communicate → recover. This would work as a group work.
  - Configure a deliberately insecure environment and gradually secure it.
  - An group work, where students play different roles and answer question "Security wants €250,000 for a new security capability." Explaining the alternatives, funding, scenarios, risks and loses for the company.

  Models of frameworks you like (if you don't know the name, you can just describe the purpose of any wouldnt-it-be-nice framework)
  - FAIR
  - CSF
  - NIST
  - Kybermittari
  - CIS Controls

  Visiting lecturers, excursions and other special events (You can describe them, don't need to name the person or company unless you know one on the top of your head)
  
  - Ethical hacker / Penetration tester
  - Digital forensics / law-enforcement specialist
  - SOC analyst / Incident responder
  - CISO or Security Manager

  Excursions:
  - Visit to a Security Operations Center (SOC)
  - Visit to a data center

  Rationale for some choices, if you want. (Need this at work, always wanted to know, my favourite approach, I believe this is the future...)
  
  - The excursions and visitors would bring a lot to the learning experience in overall.
  - The Frameworks seemed to be the ones, I found most interesting and were already somewhat part of this course.
  - The themes are important at this day and age of IT-industry, so that's why I chose them.
  - Goals came from top of my head, and I found them to be important.
